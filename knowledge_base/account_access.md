# Account Access

## I forgot my password
Go to app.northbeam.io/reset and enter your account email. A reset link
arrives within a couple of minutes -- check your spam folder if you don't
see it. Reset links expire after 1 hour for security.

## I'm not receiving the password reset email
First, confirm you're using the email address your account was originally
created with -- a different email (even a typo) will silently fail to send
anything. If you're certain the email is correct and it's been more than
10 minutes, reply to this email and we'll manually verify your account and
trigger a new reset.

## How do I add a teammate to my account?
Account owners and Admins can invite teammates from Settings > Team >
Invite Member. Invited members receive an email with a signup link valid
for 7 days. Teams on the Free plan are limited to 3 members; Pro and above
have unlimited seats.

## How do I enable two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup
flow with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or similar).
We don't currently support SMS-based 2FA.
