# Billing

## How does billing work?
Northbeam Analytics bills monthly in advance, on the same calendar day you
originally signed up. Your card on file is charged automatically -- no
invoices need to be manually paid unless you're on our annual Enterprise
plan, which is billed via invoice with NET-30 terms.

## How do I get a refund?
Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge if you haven't used more
than 10% of your plan's monthly usage quota. To request one, reply to this
email with your account email and the charge date, and our billing team
will process it within 3-5 business days.

## Can I change my plan?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing > Change Plan --
upgrades take effect immediately and you're charged a prorated difference.
Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you
keep your current plan's features until then.

## Why was I charged twice this month?
This usually happens when a plan upgrade is processed mid-cycle: you're
charged a prorated amount for the upgrade in addition to your regular
monthly charge. Both charges are legitimate, but we understand it can look
like a duplicate -- reply with your account email if you'd like us to walk
through the specific charges on your account.
